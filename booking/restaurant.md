# Sawt Bistro

## Location and contact
- 12 Olaya Street, Riyadh
- Phone: 011 555 0142

## Opening hours
- Sunday to Thursday: 12:00 to 23:00
- Friday: 13:00 to 23:30
- Saturday: 12:00 to 23:30
- Last seating is one hour before closing.

## Menu
- Starters: hummus (28 SAR), lentil soup (24 SAR), fattoush salad (32 SAR)
- Mains: grilled chicken (68 SAR), lamb kabsa (89 SAR), grilled hammour (95 SAR), vegetable tagine (62 SAR)
- Desserts: kunafa (38 SAR), date pudding (34 SAR)
- Drinks: fresh lemon mint (18 SAR), Saudi coffee (14 SAR), still or sparkling water (8 SAR)
- Vegetarian options: hummus, lentil soup, fattoush salad, vegetable tagine
- No pork and no alcohol are served.

## Reservation policies
- Reservations are for the same day only.
- Tables are for 1 to 8 guests. Parties of more than 8 must be referred to the events team at events@sawtbistro.example.
- Reservations are held for 15 minutes after the booked time.
- Cancellations are free up to 2 hours before the booking.
